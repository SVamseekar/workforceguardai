# WorkforceGuard Product Roadmap PRD

This document is the product planning entry point for WorkforceGuard.

It translates the solution architecture into phase-by-phase product requirements documents that can guide delivery, design, engineering, and stakeholder review.

## Product Summary

WorkforceGuard is a Europe-first workforce intelligence and compliance platform.

The long-term product outcome is:
- help HR, strategy, and compliance teams understand labour-market conditions across Europe
- help employers compare their internal workforce position against the market
- help teams prepare evidence-backed decisions under European regulatory expectations

## Planning Principles

- metrics before generative AI
- trust before automation
- external market intelligence before employer-specific decisioning
- evidence and provenance in every important decision flow
- honest handling of sparse or partial coverage

## How To Use These PRDs

Use each phase PRD as the contract for:
- what user problem the phase solves
- what is in scope and out of scope
- what data and system capabilities are required
- what delivery milestones matter
- what exit criteria define completion

Each phase should only begin when the previous phase is either complete or intentionally frozen.
