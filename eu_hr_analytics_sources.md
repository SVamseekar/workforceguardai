## Key Notes:

### **Data Accessibility:**
- **Fully Free & Open**: Eurostat (all datasets), Eurofound (all reports), EIGE (all indices), EURES (all dashboards), ILO ILOSTAT, McKinsey AI insights, BCG AI at Work, Bain reports (working future, HR digital), EY summaries, EU Commission documents
- **Free with Registration/Form**: Mercer reports, some Deloitte resources
- **Paywalled/Limited**: Consulting firm full reports (McKinsey, Deloitte full HCT reports)

### **EU-Specific Datasets for HR Analytics:**
- **Retention/Turnover**: Eurostat LFS flows, Eurofound EWCS job quality
- **Pay Equity**: `earn_gr_gpgr2` (gender pay gap by sector), EIGE Gender Index (power domain)
- **Housing/Affordability**: `TESSI162/164/166` (EU-SILC housing cost overburden)
- **Commuting/Mobility**: `lfso_19` (commuting time), EURES vacancy patterns by region
- **Benefits/Wellbeing**: Mercer, Deloitte Gen Z, Bain worker archetypes, EU-SILC household income
- **AI Adoption**: McKinsey State of AI, BCG AI at Work, Bain HR Digital, EY Work Reimagined

### **Region Coverage:**
- Most comprehensive for EU-27, some coverage of EFTA (Norway, Switzerland), candidate countries, and global comparisons
- UK data available through older datasets; post-Brexit separation in recent years

### **Recommended Starting Points for Product Development:**
1. **Eurostat** for official EU labour market data (free, continuous updates)
2. **Eurofound EWCS 2024** for comprehensive job quality/working conditions baseline
3. **EIGE Gender Equality Index** for pay equity & gender metrics
4. **Mercer/PwC/Deloitte reports** for benefits/engagement/wellbeing benchmarks
5. **McKinsey/BCG AI reports** for AI adoption/skills trends
6. **EURES dashboards** for real-time occupational demand signals
